The Unseen Workload

The modern teaching role extends far beyond the classroom. A significant portion of an educator's week is consumed by essential, yet non-instructional, tasks that are the primary driver of burnout.

Over 15 Hours a Week on Non-Teaching Tasks

This time is spent on a relentless cycle of preparation, administration, and communication. While vital, this "invisible workload" detracts from direct student engagement and is unsustainable, leading to an annual replacement cost of over $20,000 per teacher who leaves the profession.

  • 📋

    Lesson Planning & Differentiation: Crafting and adapting materials for diverse student needs.

  • 📝

    Grading and Feedback: Providing timely, constructive assessments for entire classes.

  • 📊

    Administrative Reporting: Documenting progress, compliance, and incidents.

  • 📧

    Parent Communication: Crafting newsletters, updates, and individual emails.

The Solution: An AI Persona Assistant

This is not another chatbot. It's a secure, specialized AI partner designed to act as a digital colleague, adopting different personas to tackle specific tasks and give educators the leverage of a support team. Click on a persona to learn more.
